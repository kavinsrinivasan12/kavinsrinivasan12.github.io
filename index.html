Bike Share User Behavior & Fleet Transit Analytics

October 2026 · Snowflake · SQL · Python

Problem: The raw bike trip records had missing station names, duplicate rides, and bad durations and coordinates, so it was hard to see how members and casual riders actually used the system or where demand peaked.

Solution: I loaded the trip data into Snowflake and cleaned it in Python, removing duplicates, trips under 1 minute or over 24 hours, and coordinates outside Chicago. I then engineered features like ride length, round-trip, commute-window and duration bands, and answered 14 business metrics in SQL using CTEs, window functions and conditional aggregation.

Impact: Turned raw trip logs into an analysis-ready Snowflake table that shows commuter vs. leisure behavior, the busiest morning stations and corridors, and which stations have the highest casual-rider share.

August 2026 · Power BI · Power Query · Data Modeling

Bank Loan Management System

August 2026 · SQL · GCP Big Query

Problem: Banks often struggle to get a clear view of loan performance and risk because the data is scattered across disconnected records, making it hard for teams to spot repayment issues or risky lending patterns before they become costly.

Solution: I built and structured an 8-table relational database with close to 305K records in BigQuery, then wrote SQL queries using CTEs, window functions, and multi-table joins to dig into borrower behavior and loan trends.

Impact: Answered 13 real business questions a bank might actually ask, giving a clearer picture of loan performance and default patterns while sharpening my SQL skills for BFSI-focused analyst roles.
